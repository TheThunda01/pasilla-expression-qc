# Maintenance and source review — 21 October 2026

This follow-up checks the existing descriptive analysis, its source interpretation, and transfer of its saved artifacts. It preserves the [20 October review](review-2026-10-20.md), original input files, and historical results. No differential-expression analysis or new biological finding is added.

## What changed

Git's automatic line-ending conversion could change saved JSON and Markdown files on Windows while leaving the manifest unchanged. `.gitattributes` now preserves every file in `results/`, along with the audit JSON, so the documented artifact check can operate on the original bytes after checkout. The analysis and stored checksums have not been replaced.

## Source reconciliation

The [Brooks et al. article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3032923/?report=xml), in Results and the Table 1 footnote, describes four depleted and three untreated cultures. The [official pasilla vignette](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/data/experiment/vignettes/pasilla/inst/doc/create_objects.html) and bundled annotation give three treated and four untreated libraries.

The original [GEO series GSE18508](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE18508) was inspected through its [SOFT archive](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE18nnn/GSE18508/soft/GSE18508_family.soft.gz). Its seven early samples include:

| Archive group | Accessions | Sample-title pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four untreated | GSM461176, GSM461177, GSM461178, GSM461182 | `S2_DRSC_Untreated` |
| Three RNAi-treated | GSM461179, GSM461180, GSM461181 | `S2_DRSC_CG8144_RNAi` |

[NCBI Gene 44258](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/44258) identifies `ps` as pasilla and lists `CG8144` as a synonym. This supports the package's allocation. The GEO series also contains many other perturbations and later untreated samples, so the entire series is not the seven-sample subset.

The exact mapping from these accessions to the renamed columns in the derived count table has not been independently rebuilt from raw data. The paper text still conflicts with the archive/package pattern. The source labels therefore remain unchanged, and treatment inference remains outside this project. This is a stronger provenance account, not a claim that every part of the original sample history is resolved.

The SampleExplorer citation retains the publisher's January 2025 issue year. Its online publication date is corrected to **30 December 2024**, as shown by the [publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/41/1/btae759/7934860) and PMC publication header. The earlier 26 December date was incorrect.
